TASK: Produce a verbatim raw transcription of everything spoken in the audio.

5. Include filler words (um, uh, ah) only if clearly audible; skip long silent gaps.
6. If a word or phrase is completely unintelligible, write [inaudible].
7. Do NOT add any commentary, explanation, or SOAP note structure — raw transcription only.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- A single continuous block of text (paragraphs separated by blank lines only if there is a clear pause/topic shift in the audio).
